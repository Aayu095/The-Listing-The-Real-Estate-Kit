One file in, every figure out

Change the asking price in src/data.ts — or a room's width, the stamp-duty rate, the loan share — and all four templates re-derive themselves: the plan redraws, room areas and the carpet total re-sum, ₹ per sq ft, carpet efficiency, the locality verdict, stamp duty, registration, the EMI and total interest all recompute. In the sample listing, ₹1.85 crore over 1,450 sq ft of carpet is ₹12,759 / sq ft — +7.2% against the Whitefield average, and the sheet says so plainly: at a premium.

The Listing is fictional. Sample data — the property, the firm, the agent, the prices and the rates are invented; the arithmetic is real.
